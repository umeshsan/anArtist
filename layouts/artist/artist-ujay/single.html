Background & journey

Born into a family of Buddhist painters in the Kathmandu valley, Ujay Bajracharya learned the intricate art of Thangka from her grandmother. She uses natural mineral pigments and 24k gold. Her thangkas are known for spiritual precision and vibrant healing iconography.

"Painting is meditation, each deity arises from silence."
