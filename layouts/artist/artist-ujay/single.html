Background & journey

This Thangka artist merges spiritual devotion with masterful technique, creating vibrant, intricate paintings that convey centuries-old cultural stories. Each work demonstrates exceptional attention to detail, precise brushwork, and symbolic richness. Through delicate layers of color and pattern, the artist preserves tradition while adding personal creativity, producing artworks that inspire reflection, admiration, and a profound connection to heritage and sacred art.

Notable works

Buddha Head Sculpture

A finely carved black stone Buddha head, showcasing delicate facial expressions and smooth, polished details.

Stone Lotus Pedestal

A traditional lotus-shaped pedestal, meticulously sculpted to highlight symmetry and natural stone texture.

Carved Guardian Figure

A striking stone guardian statue featuring intricate detailing, reflecting cultural heritage and masterful craftsmanship.
