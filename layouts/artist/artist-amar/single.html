Background & journey

We have been in the profession of stone sculpting for more than 100 years. Despite being an old profession, craftsmanship is always passed from one generation to the next, from great grandfather to his offspring. Besides sculpting rocks found in the nearby hills of Kathmandu, we also engraves on gem stones like turquoises, lapizes, and other crystal line rocks that can be cut and polished as per the demand put forwarded by customers.

Mr. Bekha Jyoti Shakya (Son of Mr. Bekha Raj Shakya) was a one of best-known Sculptor of Nepal, born on Kartik 1972 B.S at Bhinchhebahal – 07, Lalitpur, Nepal (Place of Stone Craft), the centre of stone craft. He started his work of sculpture since his early childhood. He was inspired in stone carving from his father Bekha Raj Shakya. He has acquired experience of stone carving since 1986 B.S. He was trained in stone craft by ancestral traditional way. His stone craft is based on classical style. The statues of Buddha sculpt by him have been established in Gabahal and Tapahiti, Lalitpur. He sculpts Buddha, Chaitya, Shri-Krishna, Bal Gopal and decorative items. His art itself has spread inside country cum foreign countries. He has created an idol of foreigner child. He has received a Letter of Recommendation from Lalitpur Citizen Committee in preservation of our art and heritage. One of the leading figures of our country has left the earth at the age of 83.

Embracing a family trade, Prem Ratna Shakya is the second son of Artist Bekha Jyoti Shakya who was regarded as one of the leading figures in the trade. He adopted the business not only as a means of earning, but because it plays an integral part in the preservation of art and heritage. He started sculpting stone around the age of 12 on the insistence of his father. He sculpts Buddha, Chaitya, Water spout, Steeple (Gazur), decorative items, etc., which meet the demands of businessmen and customers alike.

"Journey as a stone artist reflects patience, discipline, and respect for material."
