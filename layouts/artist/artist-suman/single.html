Background & journey

Suman Bajracharya, a stone artist living in Nepal, belongs to a land where stone carving is not just an art form but a living tradition. Nepal’s temples, stupas, and heritage sites are filled with stone sculptures that date back centuries, and artists like Amar grow up surrounded by this visual language.

From an early age, exposure to traditional architecture, religious icons, and handcrafted stone works plays a major role in shaping a stone artist’s sensitivity to form, proportion, and symbolism.

"Suman Bajracharya’s journey as a stone artist reflects patience, discipline, and respect for material."
