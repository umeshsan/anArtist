Background & journey

Santa Raj Bajracharya carries forward the legacy of Mughal-era naqqashi (metal engraving) in Lucknow. His family has worked with copper, brass and silver for over two centuries. He combines traditional floral patterns with contemporary design.

"Every curve tells a story of the past."
