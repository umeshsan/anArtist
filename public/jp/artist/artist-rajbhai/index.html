Background & journey

Renowned for transforming raw stone into timeless works of art, this stone craft artist combines precision, creativity, and tradition. Each sculpture reflects a deep understanding of texture, form, and heritage, bringing life to stone through meticulous carving. From intricate statues to functional art pieces, their work celebrates craftsmanship, cultural legacy, and the enduring beauty of natural stone, captivating audiences worldwide.
